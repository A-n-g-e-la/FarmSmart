About FarmAdvis

Local focus.
Informed decisions.

FarmAdvis is a maize farming advisory platform focused on Uasin Gishu, Kenya. Its goal is to support informed agricultural decision-making by helping farmers organize their farm information and giving approved officers a view of their assigned farmers’ records.

The current platform starts with accounts, farm-plot management, and assigned-farmer viewing. Further advisory tools are planned as the platform develops.
